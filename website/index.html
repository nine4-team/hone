Pros Understand 3 Training Principles

01Faster progress comes from focus

Your game is the sum of your skills. Focus on a few, not all of them at once.

02You can't sharpen what you can't see

You need feedback to steer your training. The clearest signal is landing a skill on a resisting opponent — a hit.

03Improvement = volume × quality

Your training time is limited, so training quality is key.

How Pros Apply These Principles

Volume is the easy part — just show up. Quality is what separates pros: they build each skill with 3 activities that supplement rolling.

01

Study

Seminars, instructionals, and footage let you learn what took someone else years in a fraction of the time.

02

Dialogue drilling

Workshopping a skill with a partner refines your understanding and helps you solve the specific problems you run into during live training.

03

Constraint games

Live games with limits let you build mat time with a skill against resistance — accumulating hits and clear feedback on what to work on.

Rolling tests your skills without constraints and lets you land hits "in the wild" — so you know what to focus on in your study, dialogue drilling, and constraint games.
