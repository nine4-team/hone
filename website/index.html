Guiding Principles

We reverse engineered 4 principles of rapid progress based on how elite grapplers train.

01Faster progress comes from focus

A skill only improves when you work on it over time. Most people train at random, so nothing gets the focus it needs.

02You need feedback to steer your training.

The clearest signal is executing a skill on a resisting opponent — a hit.

03Improvement = volume × quality

Your mat time is capped by your schedule. Quality is where the bigger gains are.

04Quality comes from deliberate practice

Elite grapplers don't just roll — they build each skill with study, dialogue drilling, and constraint games.
