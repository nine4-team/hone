Why It Works Get Started

Why do we keep showing up?

Because it's fun to hit cool, annoying, beautiful moves on our friends. Strip away everything else and that's the hobby. Skill is just how you get there.

And the honest measure of a skill is simple: can you hit it live, against someone trying to stop you? Notes, instructionals, and drilling are all inputs. The hit is the output.

So that's what HitList counts.

Getting better isn't a mystery

It comes down to two things: how much you train, and how well you train. HitList is built around both.

1

Pick a few skills

You can't deliberately improve everything at once. Choose a small set of skills worth real focus and equip them. Your Arsenal holds the rest until you're ready.

2

Train them with intent

Study them. Drill them. Run dialogue drills and live games. Roll hunting for the move on purpose. Quality practice beats logging mat hours.

3

Sustain the focus

Improvement compounds when you keep pointing at the same skills, week after week. The more often you train, the faster it comes.

4

Measure it in hits

Forget stripes and gym hours. Count the moves you land live, against resistance. That number tells you the truth — and tells you what to work on next.
